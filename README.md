# Link Decor - Interior Solutions Karachi

High-converting lead generation website for **Link Decor**, premier interior design and contracting specialists in Karachi, Pakistan.

## Core Specializations

- **Gypsum & PVC False Ceilings** (`/services/ceilings`): Moisture-resistant Gypsum boards, contemporary cove lighting, ambient LED grooves, and suspended PVC tiles starting from Rs. 140/sq.ft.
- **Wooden, Vinyl & SPC Flooring** (`/services/flooring`): 100% waterproof Stone Plastic Composite (SPC) click-lock planks, German laminate wood, and luxury vinyl planks starting from Rs. 280/sq.ft.
- **3D & Fabric Wallpapers** (`/services/wallpapers`): Deep embossed 3D textures, luxury velvet fabrics, and custom wall murals starting from Rs. 70/sq.ft.
- **WPC & PVC Wall Paneling** (`/services/wall-paneling`): Fluted wood-plastic composite (WPC) louvers, UV high-gloss marble sheets, and acoustic wooden slats starting from Rs. 220/sq.ft.
- **Window Blinds & Glass Films** (`/services/window-blinds`): Made-to-measure Zebra day-and-night blinds, blackout roller blinds, and frosted privacy glass films starting from Rs. 160/sq.ft.

## Conversion & Lead Generation Architecture

- **Consultative WhatsApp Engine**: Direct-to-WhatsApp pre-filled dynamic messages customized by service category and location.
- **On-Site Estimation Form**: Dynamic quotation calculator capturing client name, Karachi district/area, room size (sq.ft), and requirements.
- **Local SEO & Schema Markup**: Rich local business metadata, breadcrumbs, service schema, and targeted Karachi keywords (`Best False Ceiling in Karachi`, etc.).
