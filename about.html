About Link Decor – Karachi's Trusted Interior Specialists

Over a decade of transforming residences, executive offices, and luxury retail spaces across Karachi through precision installation, honest square-foot rates, and dedicated master craftsmen.

Who We Are

Crafting Enduring Interiors Built for Karachi

Founded with a commitment to eliminate inflated intermediary charges in the contracting industry, Link Decor has emerged as one of Karachi's leading providers of specialized interior solutions. Headquartered with physical operations in Baldia Town, Karachi, we dispatch specialized teams across every major district of the city.

From luxury bungalows in DHA and Clifton to high-rise apartments in Gulshan-e-Iqbal and modern corporate offices, we understand the distinct environmental demands of Karachi—from salt-laden humidity and sub-floor seepage to intense solar heat.

Every material we recommend—be it water-resistant Gypsum board, rigid-core SPC flooring, or UV-resistant PVC wall panels—is tested to perform reliably in our local climate.
