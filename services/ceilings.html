Durable, Anti-Crack False Ceilings Built for Karachi Climate

Humidity and sea-breeze moisture in Karachi often cause inferior gypsum ceilings to sag or develop hairline cracks. At Link Decor, we construct our framing strictly using 24-gauge galvanized iron (GI) channels with rust-proof coating and authentic Boral / Elephant Gypsum boards.

For areas prone to dampness such as kitchens, washroom ceilings, and covered balconies, we install tongue-and-groove waterproof PVC ceiling panels that eliminate peeling paint and mold forever.

  • check_circle Concealed Ambient Lighting: Seamless LED cove steps and magnetic track light cutouts.
  • check_circle Thermal & Acoustic Insulation: Lowers room temperatures and reduces Karachi summer cooling bills.
  • check_circle Level-5 Smooth Putty Finish: Ready for emulsion, matt or texture paint.
