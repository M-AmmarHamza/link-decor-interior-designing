Waterproof Luxury

Engineered for Karachi Coastal Humidity & Heavy Daily Foot Traffic

Traditional wooden parquet flooring often swells, warps, or attracts termites when exposed to Karachi's coastal air and sub-floor seepage. At Link Decor, we specialize in high-density Stone Plastic Composite (SPC) rigid flooring.

Formulated from natural limestone powder and virgin polymers, our SPC planks do not expand, warp, or absorb liquid. Fitted with the patented German Uniclic tongue-and-groove system, they install swiftly over existing marble or tile floors with zero mess and zero messy glue.

  • check_circle 100% Waterproof & Termite-Proof: Wash with wet mops safely without swelling.
  • check_circle Pre-Attached IXPE Foam: Cushioned underfoot feel with superior acoustic sound deadening.
  • check_circle Realistic Embossed Grain: Authentic oak, walnut, teak, and herringbone textures.
