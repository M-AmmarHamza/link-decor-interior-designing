Terms and Conditions

Last updated: October 2026

1. Estimations and Site Measurements

Initial estimates provided via WhatsApp or online calculators are non-binding ballpark assessments based on user-provided room dimensions. Formal contract pricing is confirmed only following an authorized on-site physical measurement by a Link Decor supervisor in Karachi.

2. Quotation Validity & Material Procurement

Due to raw material market dynamics in Pakistan, written quotations remain valid for a period of 15 calendar days from issuance. Material procurement commences upon mutual agreement of the Bill of Quantities (BOQ) and mobilization terms.

3. Installation & Site Readiness

The client agrees to provide reasonable site access, continuous electrical power, and adequate lighting during active installation hours. Sub-floors and masonry walls must be structurally stable and free of active plumbing leaks.

4. Craftsmanship Warranty

Link Decor provides a warranty against installation defects on Gypsum framing, SPC floor alignment, and WPC clip attachments as specified in the agreed project handover document.
